## How to use this vault

- Product, strategy, roadmap, research, launch notes, and agent context live here.
- The repo may keep only minimal code-facing setup notes and pointers back to this vault.
- Keep secrets out of notes. Credential-like values from migrated files were conservatively redacted where detected.
