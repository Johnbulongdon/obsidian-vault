# Guest Post Outreach — Backlinks via FIRE/Personal-Finance Blogs

Goal: earn genuine backlinks and referral traffic by writing useful guest posts for FIRE and personal-finance blogs. This replaces the Reddit/X/HN launch-post plan as the primary acquisition push (John's call, 2026-07-02).

Why this matters for $3k MRR: untilfire.com has almost no sustained acquisition channel (90-day PostHog data: traffic is ~all direct). Guest posts compound: each one gives a backlink (domain authority → city/calculator SEO pages start ranking) plus a warm referral audience that already cares about FIRE.

## Rules

- Honest founder voice. John is a builder sharing what he learned making a FIRE tool — not an SEO vendor.
- Never pay for links or use "guest post service" marketplaces. Paid-link schemes violate Google spam policy and can kill the domain before it has authority.
- Every pitch offers a real article idea tailored to that blog's audience, not a generic template blast.
- One contextual link to untilfire.com (or a city/calculator page) inside the article where it genuinely helps the reader; author-bio link as fallback if editorial policy restricts in-content links.
- Follow each blog's guidelines exactly (word count, format, exclusivity). FIRE editors reject template spam on sight.

## Pitch angles (pick per audience)

1. **The freedom-date reframe.** "Your FIRE number is a poor motivator — a freedom *date* changes behavior." What we learned building a calculator that leads with the date, not the number.
2. **City math.** "Your FIRE number depends more on your zip code than your salary" — real cost-of-living/tax spreads across 263 cities (great for PoF/high-earner and UK/international blogs).
3. **Late starters.** "Starting FIRE at 45: what the math actually says" — compound runway data; perfect for Catching Up to FI.
4. **Builder story.** "I built a FIRE calculator and watched 100 people use it — here's where they gave up" — honest funnel data storytelling; perfect for interview formats and Fiology.
5. **The one monthly move.** "Stop optimizing everything: the single monthly action that moves a freedom date most" — savings-rate sensitivity analysis.
