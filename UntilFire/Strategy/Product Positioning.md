# UntilFire — Product Positioning

## One-line positioning

UntilFire is personal finance that sets you free: a calm financial freedom app that shows your freedom date, then helps you move it closer with a clear plan.

## Short startup description

UntilFire is a personal finance product that helps people move toward financial freedom by showing where they stand, what actions matter most, and how to make work optional over time.

It is interesting because instead of just tracking money or giving a one-time answer, it gives users a clear path that turns financial progress into something practical, motivating, and easier to act on.

## Core positioning statement

UntilFire is for people who want more freedom from work and money stress, but do not want to become spreadsheet experts or amateur financial planners just to get there.

Most personal finance tools do one of two things: they track what already happened, or they give you a number and leave you alone. UntilFire is different. It starts with a simple, motivating value moment — your freedom date — then turns that result into practical next steps you can actually follow.

The product is not trying to be a generic budgeting app, a dense FIRE simulator, or a wealth manager. It is a practical guide for people who want to understand where they stand, what matters most, and what to do next.

## The problem

People feel financially aware, but not financially clear.

They already have banking apps, scattered notes, spreadsheets, and advice from the internet. But they still do not know:

- if they are on track
- how much is enough
- what they should do this month
- whether they should focus on spending, income, or investing first
- how their choices affect their path to freedom

For many people, existing finance tools create one of two bad outcomes:

1. **Too shallow** — they show balances and categories, but do not tell you what to do.
2. **Too heavy** — they ask for too much setup, too many assumptions, and too much financial confidence before delivering value.

## See the gap

There is a gap between a calculator and an adviser.

- A calculator gives you a result.
- A tracker gives you a record.
- A simulator gives you a model.
- An adviser gives you a path.

That gap is where UntilFire lives.

Most FIRE tools are built for people who already speak the language, already trust the assumptions, and already have the patience to model everything themselves. Most mainstream finance apps are built around budgets, accounts, and dashboards, not around the emotional question users actually care about: *when does money stop controlling my life?*

UntilFire bridges that gap by combining three things in one experience:

1. **A fast freedom-date answer** — clear value without friction.
2. **A simple, trustworthy picture of the user’s situation** — grounded in real cities, taxes, and context.
3. **A practical next-step plan** — guidance that helps the user act, not just observe.

## Who it is for

UntilFire is built first for financially aware but under-guided people:

- people who think about freedom, retirement, or money stress often
- people who want clarity without complexity
- people who need help understanding what matters most right now
- people who are early in their planning journey, not only advanced FIRE hobbyists
- global and cross-border users who feel overlooked by US-only tools

In practice, the strongest early user groups are:

- investment-confidence seekers
- income climbers
- global / cross-border planners
- early-career stabilizers
- FIRE-curious planners

## Category

UntilFire should be positioned as a **financial freedom app**.

Not:

- a generic budget tracker
- a retirement calculator
- a finance dashboard
- a wealth manager
- a tool that only helps after you already know what you are doing

## What users should feel

After using UntilFire, the user should feel:

- *I finally know where I stand.*
- *I can see a path, not just a number.*
- *This feels simple enough to trust and useful enough to come back to.*
- *This helps me move toward freedom, not just stare at my finances.*

## Product promise

UntilFire helps you understand your path to financial freedom and gives you clear next moves to bring that freedom closer.

## Differentiation

### Versus budgeting apps
Budgeting apps help users record and categorize money. UntilFire helps users connect money decisions to freedom and decide what to do next.

### Versus FIRE calculators
FIRE calculators help users estimate a target or timeline. UntilFire starts there, but does not stop there — it continues into guidance, progress, and practical planning.

### Versus advanced simulators
Advanced simulators are powerful, but often too heavy for first value. UntilFire is faster, simpler, and more emotionally legible for people who want clarity before complexity.

### Versus human advisers
Human advisers are expensive and often inaccessible for younger or earlier-stage users. UntilFire aims to give people a lightweight, affordable sense of guidance before they would ever hire an adviser.

## Messaging guardrails

- Lead with freedom from financial stress and work, and with a path.
- Start with the freedom date because it is concrete and motivating.
- Emphasize guidance and next moves, not just analysis.
- Keep the tone calm, practical, and non-judgmental.
- Use city/tax accuracy and global coverage as trust proof, not the headline.
- Avoid sounding like a generic tracker, spreadsheet replacement, or finance-content hub.
- Avoid assuming the user already identifies as “FIRE.” Many users want the outcome before they adopt the label.

## Roadmap implication

If this positioning is true, the roadmap should prioritize:

1. faster trust-building to the freedom-date moment
2. clearer post-result guidance
3. practical monthly next moves
4. simple explanations for investing, savings, and income trade-offs
5. global / tax-aware trust proof where it meaningfully changes confidence, without making international breadth the main story yet

It should deprioritize:

- broad dashboard complexity without guidance
- feature sprawl in generic tracking
- power-user modeling before beginner clarity
- early paywall pressure before value is felt

## Short internal version

UntilFire is not a calculator that stops at the answer.
It is the product that gives people their freedom date, then helps them move it closer.

## Current positioning decisions

1. Use the broader category: **financial freedom app**.
2. Treat freedom from financial stress and freedom from work as the same core emotional outcome.
3. Keep global / cross-border strength as supporting trust proof for now, not the lead positioning.
