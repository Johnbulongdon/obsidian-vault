# Pricing and Pro Packaging

## Current pricing

UntilFire Pro is currently priced at **$4.99/month**.

John updated the Stripe price ID in Vercel on 2026-05-20. Keep the actual Stripe price ID out of notes and logs.

## Current beta posture

Pricing infrastructure can be ready, but Pro should stay soft during friends-and-family beta.

During beta:

- Do not push payment before the user receives value.
- Do not interrupt the no-login freedom-date result with a hard paywall.
- Reward useful early supporters with perks such as free Pro or lifetime access where appropriate.
- Treat the $4.99 price as a lightweight early Pro price, not the main launch story.

## Packaging direction

Pro should be framed around helping users know what to do next, not generic dashboard access.

Likely Pro value:

- AI financial coach.
- Personalized monthly moves.
- Investment projections.
- Personalized FIRE roadmap.
- Ongoing progress insights.

Avoid making Pro feel like:

- A budgeting app clone.
- A login-first experience.
- A bank-connection requirement.
- A scary financial advice product.

## Survey support

The friends beta survey supports this lower-price posture:

- 60% said maybe they would pay for a tool that clearly improved financial planning.
- 40% said yes under $5/month.
- 0% selected $5–$15/month or $15+/month in the small sample.
