# Sprint 18 — Monthly Discipline Loop

**Status:** 🔲 Planned  
**Module:** Dashboard → Monthly Guidance Loop  
**Phase mapping:** Phase 3 — Build the monthly discipline loop  
**Goal:** Solve “staying disciplined” with the smallest useful system.

## Problem

Users may understand their freedom date but still struggle to keep going month after month. The product needs a simple system that helps users:

- know this month’s plan
- check whether they followed it
- recover calmly if they missed a month
- feel progress, not guilt

Without this loop, UntilFire risks feeling like a one-time answer instead of ongoing support.

## User story

As a user trying to get closer to financial freedom, I want a simple monthly plan and check-in flow — so I can stay consistent without feeling overwhelmed or judged.

## Done when

- [ ] The product shows a simple **This month’s plan** card or section.
- [ ] The monthly plan includes:
  - target contribution
  - 1–3 monthly moves
  - status / progress
- [ ] Users can complete a simple check-in, such as:
  - did you make your planned contribution?
  - did anything change this month?
- [ ] The product includes a calm recovery flow if the user misses a month.
- [ ] Recovery flow avoids guilt language and suggests one smaller next step.
- [ ] Progress reinforcement exists, such as:
  - consistency / streak
  - months completed
  - freedom date moved closer
- [ ] Users can understand one clear monthly plan without digging through multiple tabs.
- [ ] Missing a month does not make the experience feel broken or punitive.

## Acceptance criteria

- The monthly loop feels behavioral and supportive, not like homework.
- The first plan is simple enough for a beginner to follow.
- Check-in can be completed quickly.
- The product reinforces progress even when movement is small.
- The experience stays calm on mobile and does not add dashboard noise.

## Out of scope

- Full AI adviser personalization engine
- Notifications-first retention systems before the loop is clear
- Complex habit frameworks or gamification systems
- Deep portfolio optimization logic
